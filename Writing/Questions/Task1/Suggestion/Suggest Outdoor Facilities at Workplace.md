Dear Mr. Lee,

I am writing to express my concern regarding the inadequate lighting and damaged stairway at the entrance to our company. I hope these issues can be addressed promptly.

Whenever I walk through the courtyard outside our building at night, I find this area hazardous due to insufficient illumination and uneven stairs. Poor visibility increases the likelihood of tripping on deteriorating steps, posing a significant safety threat. Furthermore, the dim lighting negatively impacts our company's public image.

To address these issues, I recommend having the relevant maintenance or facilities team inspect the current outdoor lighting system. Some of the LED units seem to be malfunctioning and require replacement. The team should also evaluate whether a full lighting upgrade is necessary. Additionally, the front stairs must be repaired to ensure safety.

If these issues were resolved, employees would benefit from better visibility, enabling them to navigate this area more safely. Beyond safety, well-maintained outdoor lighting would create a more professional exterior, fostering a greater sense of pride among staff and boosting overall morale.

As these improvements affect the safety of all staff members, your prompt assistance in this matter would be highly appreciated.

Yours sincerely,

Jeff Ho


* facilities => 2 個以上的設施
* You work at this company, so you should invent a name for your manager. => 知道對方姓名的formal letter. => Yours sincerely,
* lodge a complaint about (提起正式的法律訴訟，富有嚴重的警告意味) => bring an issue regarding
* the LED illumination system (太技術性了) => the outdoor lighting system, the LED lighting system
* "has been malfunctioning"
* "For unknown reasons" 此為固定用法，在某些特殊語境下甚至可以省略
* "on foggy nights" 此為固定用法，nights複數表示一般一再重複的情況
* individuals who walked through 時態錯誤 => 燈泡直到寫信的當下還是壞的，陳述現在事實 => 現在式
=> anyone walking through this area is more likely to trip on the stairs. 在階梯上面絆倒
* "trip on the steps" (steps 為戶外的台階, stairs 為室內的樓梯)
* "oncoming cars" 迎面而來的車輛 / oncoming traffic
* "I hope you could contact ..." (建議語氣較弱)，當寫到實際應該要怎麼做時語氣可以明確一點，如：I would suggest/recommend + Ving => I would recommend contacting ... 或是 Could we contact ...? 問句結尾。
* "contact the relevant maintenance or support teams" 知會聯絡相關支援維修部門 or "call an electrician" 打電話叫電工來修繕
* check => inspect 檢查
* Maintaining this area's illumination ... "Ving" 的句型架構是用來解釋或是陳述一般明顯的事實給已經知道情況的人，在上下文都是建議的情形是，此句話會中斷讀者的閱讀節奏，因為他們預期看到的都是建議要怎麼做。
=> I would be grateful if you could request them to replace the broken LEDs.  想到broken就是要用replace
* request sb to do sth => have sb do sth (have them replace).
* could have clear views (用在看風景的時候，指良好視野) => would have better visibility (用在光線以及安全性，指良好視線)
* "Drivers could also see pedestrians from a distance and slow down accordingly."
* 為了和前一句employees would have better ... 的架構排比平行，下一句的用詞 could => would be able to 在修辭上會比較好。
* "As this issue affects everyone's safety, your prompt assistance in this matter would be highly appreciated."
* 驚嘆號不能出現在formal and semi-formal letters 裡。
