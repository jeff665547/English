【IELTS雅思8.5分】英文寫作Writing Task 1圖表分析必用Vocab句式

迅狐分享一些IELTS Academic Paper寫作部分Writing Task 1圖表分析題非常有用，而且差不多每題都能用到的超實用Vocab及句式。


圖表分析－形容詞adj/ 副詞adv

    gradual/ moderate/ modest/ slight (小幅度)

    sharp/ dramatic/ steep/ significant/ rapid/ considerable (大幅度)

    unchanged/ steady/ flat (平坦)

    marginally/ slightly/ considerably/ sharply/ dramatically/ relatively constant/ merely/ a

    mere/ climbing steadily（形容升跌幅度）

    approximately （大約）

    Likewise （相似地）

    whereas/ compared to/ as opposed to/ in comparison with（相對地）

圖表分析－動詞verb

    rise/grow/ climb/ increase/ escalate(升)

    jump/ soar/ surge/ rocket/ skyrocket (大升)

    fall/ drop/ decline/ decrease/ go down/ shrink (跌)

    plummet/ plunge (大跌)

    halve (減半)、double (雙倍化)、treble (三倍化)

    together accounted for/ represented xx%（共佔多少）

圖表分析－名詞noun

    growth/ increase/ rise (升)

    decline/ decrease/ drop/ fall/ dip(跌)

    fluctuation (波動)

    a significant majority (大多數)

    a small minority (極少數)

    nearly three quarters (近四分三)

    roughly two thirds (約三分二)

    just over a half (剛好過半)

    just under a half (剛好少過一半)

    reached a high/peak of xxx, remained at xxx （高峰）

Connectives連接詞/ 句式

    To begin with/ To start with/ To kick off/ Firstly (首先)

    In addition/ Moreover/ Furthermore (再者)

    At the start of the period/ Initially, … and following that

    there is an increase = an increase can be seen/observed

    For example/ For instance/ are prime examples/ typical examples are/ To illustrate (舉例)

    As a result/ Consequently/ Therefore/ Hence/ Thus/ For this reason (因此)

    In terms of…/ With regards to… （關於/至於xxx）

    In particular/ Specifically (當中/尤其…) (進一步解釋)

    However/ Nevertheless/ Nontheless (然而)

    On the contrary/ In stark contrast (相反)

    In conclusion/ To summarise/ All in all (總結而言)
