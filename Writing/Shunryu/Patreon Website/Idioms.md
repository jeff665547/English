【英文vocab】英文idioms 中文成語 對照表 - 隔籬飯香 石頭砸腳 英文怎說？

1. 隔籬飯香 ＝ the grass is always greener on the other side  

2. 一朝被蛇咬，十年怕草繩 = once bitten, twice shy 

3. 一言既出，駟馬難追 = a word spoken is past recalling 

4. 一箭雙鵰 ＝ kill two birds with one stone 

5. 一講曹操，曹操就到  ＝  speak of the devil

6. 搬石頭砸自己的腳 ＝ shoot yourself in the foot

7. 豬乸會上樹 ＝ when pigs fly 

8. 入境隨俗  = when in Rome (do as the Romans do) 

9. 物以類聚 人以群分 = Birds of a feather (flock together) 

10. 一粒老鼠屎，壞了一鍋湯 ＝ The rotten apple injures its neighbour
