英文進階字詞句式 (1) - 身體部分作為動詞？shoulder不只是膊頭？

    很多人在寫／講英文時，往往會因為字詞不足而詞不達意。考試的時候，也因字詞句式貧乏，來來去去都用一些基本字詞，因而在lexical resources一項被狠狠扣分。這類問題，我們稱之為「詞窮」。

    「必學英文進階字詞句式 」系列，就是為大家解決「詞窮」的問題。這個新系列出現的字詞，都是在較多情境能使用到的，我們稱之為「百搭」。

    目標：每篇學習同一個主題的5組字詞句式（vocab/ phrases/ sentence structures) ，配合例句加強學習效果。


1. shoulder (v.) 肩負/ 承擔

(a)  shoulder the responsibility 肩負起作為xxx的責任

e.g. He said Hong Kong should shoulder （肩負） the responsibility as a member of the “global village”, adding that details will be discussed with the two power companies in due course. 

(b) shoulder the blame 承擔指責

e.g. His teammates wouldn't let the young QB shoulder （承擔） all the blame. 


2. shoulder to shoulder (adv phrase) (擠迫/緊密合作)

(a) Everyone was squeezed shoulder to shoulder (擠迫) on the crowded train. 

(b) I stand shoulder to shoulder (緊密合作) with the teammates in this effort.

3. turn a blind eye to = turn a deaf ear to (v. phrase) 無視

從字面看，用盲眼望向，用聾耳去聽，很明顯就是無視的意思。

e.g. The authority has turned a deaf ear to/ turned a blind eye to/ neglected/ ignored (無視) the people's demands.


4. keep a cool head (v. phrase) 保持冷靜

e.g. It is important to keep a cool head (保持冷靜) in an emergency.


5. point an accusing finger at (v. phrase) 指責

The governor pointed an accusing finger at (指責) the legislature for failing to approve her budget.
