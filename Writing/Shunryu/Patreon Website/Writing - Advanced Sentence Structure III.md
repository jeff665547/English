英文進階字詞句式 (3) - 把單詞修飾成phrase，美化句子

英文寫作，尤其英式英文，好多時可以透過轉換詞性把單詞(vocab)修飾成短語(phrase)，去美化句子，令文章更為生動多變。舉例:


1. ends (vi) --> comes to an end

e.g. The cold war ended. --> The cold war came to an end.


2. ends (vt) --> puts an end to 

e.g. The government has introduced enhanced legislation that aims to put an end to the animal cruelty.


3.  try sth --> give sth a try

e.g. He is planning to try skiing. --> He is planning to give skiing a try.


4. starts/ kicks off --> marks the start of 

e.g. Black Friday traditionally marks the start of the Christmas shopping season.


5. emphasise --> place an emphasis on

e.g. The authority should place an emphasis on narrowing the gender gap.
