【英文vocab】中文常用 但英文不會寫的 進階字詞 (1)

indomitable (adj) 不屈不撓的

e.g. Only with an indomitable spirit can we overcome the hurdles in life and keep going in pursuit of our goals.


on the verge of ... 瀕臨

e.g. This company is on the verge of bankruptcy.


embody(v) 體現

e.g. He embodied good sportsmanship on the football field.


reinvigorate(v) 使恢復元氣 ;  moribund (adj) 停滯不前的

e.g. Changing its CEO might help reinvigorate the moribund company.


cusp (n) 分界線 

e.g. Year 2020 may be the cusp of a brave or terrifying new world.


shopworn (adj) 過氣的

e.g. Sometimes we should cast away the shopworn practices and do things in an innovative way.


extravagant (adj) 奢侈的

e.g. The extravagant use of packaging on many products intensifies environmental pollution.


exasperation (n) 憤怒

e.g. There is growing exasperation within the government at the failure of these policies to reduce unemployment.


dwarf (v) 使…相形見絀

e.g. The valuation of Tesla drawfs those of traditional US automakers.


Wreak (v) 引起(災難);  havoc(n) 破壞

e.g. The typhoon wreaked havoc in the town.
