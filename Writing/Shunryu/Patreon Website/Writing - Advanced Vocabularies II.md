【英文vocab】中文常用 但英文不會寫的 進階字詞(2)

stymie (v)妨礙 

e.g. In our search for evidence, we were stymied by the absence of any recent documents.


pernicious (adj) = harmful

e.g. The cuts in government funding have had a pernicious effect on local health services.


jostle (v) 爭先

e.g. Auto companies are jostling to invest billions in electric vehicles. 


about-face/about turn (n) 180度轉

e.g. Their about-face was driven by the threat of their competitor.


dawning (adj) 逐漸變得明朗的

e.g. There is a dawning realization that we cannot continue to deplete the planet.


boondoggle(n) 大白象;    gargantuan (adj) = massive = enormous 

The new highways proposal is regarded as a boondoggle that will use a gargantuan amount of taxpayers' money.


pander to (v) 迎合；逢迎

Musk has faced criticism for pandering to America's increasingly assertive authoritarian rival.

while away sth (v) 消磨時間

We whiled away the afternoon playing cards.


stumbling block (n) 絆腳石


distraught (adj) = extremely worried
