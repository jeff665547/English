Writing Task 2 技巧全攻略 + 實題示範

IELTS雅思寫作部分Writing Task 2，俗稱「大作文」。顧名思義，相對於Task 1，Writing Task 2佔分較多(66.7%)，題目要求寫一篇formal的議論文， 建議字數較多(250–300 字)，建議用時較長(40–45 min)。


迅狐建議，Task 2時間分配為：用5分鐘審題+寫大綱，35–40分鐘寫作。

內容方面，分為4至5段寫：Introduction + 2–3 main points + Conclusion

    建議寫法：(一) Introduction + (二) 2–3 main points + (三) Conclusion


一、引言Introduction (2句) (<50字)

(1) 1句：背景（background statement）= 把題目paraphrase一次

(2) 1句：立論（thesis statement) = 簡介自己立場


看看官方答案:

(1) Given the power and influence of the super rich, it might seem as if social status and material possessions are thenew symbols of personal worth, （用不同字眼把題目paraphrase一次）


(2) but in everyday life I do not think this is true（簡介自己立場）.這裡用了paraphrasing手法，把“person’s worth seems to be judged by X and Y” 改成 “it might seem as if X and Y are the new symbols of personal worth”


注意！有人會想把social status和material possessions也嘗試paraphrase一下，例如改成materialism (物質主義)。小心！對於專有名詞，要很小心一些相近而非相同的概念，例如town(小鎮) vs city(城市) vs metropolitan(大都會)，vegetarian(仍會吃蛋/奶的素食者) vs vegan (完全素食者)。


如果覺得有點詞窮，想不到怎樣做paraphrasing，可以參考另一篇Paraphrasing 4式。只要有意識有技巧去做，其實不難。



二、2–3個Main Paragraphs（每段4–6句，一段一論點）

上述官方Marking scheme清楚說明，內容上需要有‘extended and well supported ideas’，那麼如何做到extended and well supported呢？給考官看看你的補充說明、例子和小結吧。


技巧上，可以利用以下公式去組成一個段落：

(1) Topic Sentence 主題句 

+ (2) Background Information補充說明/Elaboration 進一步解釋 

+ (3) Examples 例子

+ (4) Concluding Sentence 收結句


官方答案拆解：

It is apparent that most celebrities today are admired or envied solely for their material wealth or position in various social hierarchies. （Topic Sentence 主題句：直接把論點最核心的中心思想說出）


Many of these people are known to turn their backs on friends, cheat on their spouses or spend their evenings over-indulging in alcohol and/or drugs. （Background Information補充說明/Elaboration 進一步解釋：通常是講解一下事情的背景，或解釋一下Topic Sentence）


Things like owning a mansion, driving an expensive car and getting into A-list parties are exalted above old-fashioned values. (Examples 例子：把抽象事情具體化。除了一般用”For instance,”/“For example,”開首舉例，也可以像這裡用”Things like A, B C and D”作為subject開首的方式舉例。如果一時間想不到實例（如Paris Hilton等有錢而私生活混亂的名人），就給出一些設例、史例、或如上面這些憑空想像出來的例子，都可做到具體化的效果。）


Ultimately, though, it is the many readers of gossip magazines and celebrity blogs who reinforce these ideas.（Concluding Sentence 收結句: 可以總結這段所說的，也可以展望將來，也可以像上面一樣把現象歸因於某些因素）



三、總結 Conclusion (2–3句)

到了總結階段，不要再加入新的內容！！！只需簡潔summarise一次內文的2–3個論點，為免重覆字眼，盡量要用parahrasing方法改頭換面，順便顯示vocab多元化的功力。


官方答案：In conclusion, I believe there is some truth to the notion that status and possessions have superseded old-fashioned values as a measure of a person’s worth. Looking beyond the tabloids, however, it is apparent that most ordinary people have still preserved an old-fashioned conscience.
